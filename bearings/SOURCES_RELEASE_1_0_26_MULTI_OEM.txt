SKF
- Existing LUBRICA SKF bearing and insert-bearing references remain in the project. SKF generic side/groove relubrication methods are retained as selectable methods and engineering fallback when no exact OEM dataset exists.

IMPORTANT
- LUBRICA never treats a generic/standard envelope as manufacturer-specific OEM internal geometry.
- Exact OEM data are marked OEM exact.
- Manufacturer method without an exact per-bearing table is marked OEM method.
- Geometry/mass free-space estimation is marked Engineering fallback.
- Final approval remains an engineering action.
