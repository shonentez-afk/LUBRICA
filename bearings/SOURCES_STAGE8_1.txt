Stage 8.1 calculation model correction

1. Bearing mass is no longer an input for full grease replacement.
2. Lubrica calculates the geometric annular envelope from dimensions:
   Vgeom = π/4 × (D² − d²) × B × 10⁻³ [cm³].
3. The actual grease-containing free volume is not identical to the geometric envelope.
   Therefore the application requires an explicit free-space coefficient Kfree (0…1).
4. Grease volume:
   Vgrease = Vgeom × Kfree × Kfill.
5. Grease mass:
   G = Vgrease × grease density.
6. Kfree is intentionally not hard-coded as one universal constant. It must come from the bearing/OEM data or an approved engineering rule.
7. If Kfree is unknown, Lubrica shows Vgeom but does not invent an exact full-fill mass.

The relubrication formulas from Stage 8 remain separate from full replacement.
