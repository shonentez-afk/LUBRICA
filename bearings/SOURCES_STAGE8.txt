Stage 8 calculation basis

SKF:
- Relubrication from the side: Gp = 0.005 × D × B [g]
- Relubrication through annular groove / lubrication holes: Gp = 0.002 × D × B [g]
- Relubrication interval adjustments: halve for each 15 °C above 70 °C; vertical shaft halves interval; intervals above 30 000 h are not recommended.
- Bearing free volume formula uses d, D, B and bearing mass.

Schaeffler:
- Replenishment m1 = D × B × x [g]
  weekly x=0.002, monthly x=0.003, yearly x=0.004.

Design rule in Lubrica:
- The app does NOT approximate the SKF relubrication interval chart.
- User supplies the base interval from SKF/OEM/approved engineering source; Stage 8 applies only explicit generic adjustment rules.
- Vibration, severe contamination and heavy-load reductions are warnings unless an engineer supplies a condition factor.
- Calculated values and approved values are stored separately.
