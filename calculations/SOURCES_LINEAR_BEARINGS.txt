Stage 8.3 — Linear Bearings calculation basis

THK LM Guide:
- General reference relubrication interval: approximately every 100 km of travel distance (three to six months).
- Final lubrication interval and amount must be established on the actual machine.
- Orientation and operating conditions affect lubricant distribution.

HIWIN linear guideways:
- Relubrication quantity is specified by guide series/size in manufacturer tables.
- High loads, vibration and dirt shorten relubrication intervals.
- For vertical, side or profile-rail-up installation, relubrication quantities are increased by approximately 50%.
- Short stroke applications require special treatment; the HIWIN instruction references initial-lubrication procedures.

Lubrica Stage 8.3 rules:
1. No universal D×B or geometry formula is used for linear guides.
2. Calculated relubrication quantity requires an OEM quantity per carriage / bushing.
3. Total quantity = OEM quantity per unit × number of units × explicitly selected orientation factor.
4. HIWIN +50% is applied only when the user explicitly selects the HIWIN orientation rule.
5. THK 100 km / 3–6 months is an optional reference source, never an automatically approved interval.
6. Calculated and approved values remain separate.
