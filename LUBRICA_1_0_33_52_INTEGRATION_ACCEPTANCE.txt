NOTES
- Existing calculation formulas were not changed.
- Sealed bearing recognition was extended so BS2-2208-2CS/VT143 does not get treated as an ordinary open spherical roller bearing.
- SKF documentation states sealed spherical roller bearings with VT143 use SKF LGEP 2 factory grease and are normally virtually maintenance-free; application-specific relubrication still requires OEM/installation confirmation.
