1.0.33.2 rules:
- Exact DE/NDE is localized only where the cited ABB table supports the family/frame/pole combination.
- M2CA 280/315 uses standard regreasing policy from ABB steel catalogue; L1 values are then selected from the ABB motor manual.
- M3AA 160–250 is treated as permanently lubricated shielded construction in the cited 2022 catalogue; M3AA 280 is regreasable.
- M2QA 71–132 is treated as sealed 2RS construction; exact bearing designation is still read from the rating plate.
- Machine-specific rating plate and special variant always override family mapping.
