LUBRICA 1.0.33.5 — Remaining Motor Fleet OEM Sources

1. OEMER QL/HQL/HQLa catalogue
URL: https://oemerspa.com/OEMER%20Download%20Center/Catalogues/7.1.0_QL_HQL_HQLa.pdf
Use: HQL 160 standard bearing construction. Catalogue bearing table lists DE 6312ZZC3 and NDE 6311ZZC3 for HQL 160. Exploded view for HQL 80–160 does not show grease valves; larger HQL/HQLa 180–280 exploded view explicitly shows grease valves. LUBRICA therefore treats the standard HQL160 configuration as a construction-based no-routine-relube policy. Special bearing options require exact motor evidence.
Evidence class: B/C — OEM product construction + explicit LUBRICA maintenance inference.

2. Interroll Drum Motor catalogue — 138i
URL: https://www.interroll.com/fileadmin/user_upload/Downloads__PDF_/Drives___Controls/Drum_Motors/Drum_Motor_Catalog_EN.pdf
Use: Interroll 138i is stated as maintenance-free and lifetime lubricated. No routine bearing relubrication task is generated.
Evidence class: A — explicit OEM product data.
